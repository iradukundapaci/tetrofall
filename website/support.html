Frequently asked questions

Why do I see an ad?
Tetrofall is free to play and supported by ads. Some ads (like the continue option) are entirely optional and only shown if you choose to watch them.
Does Tetrofall work offline?
Yes — core gameplay works without an internet connection. Ads require an internet connection to load.
How is my high score tracked?
Your best score is saved on your device and compared against the global high-score challenge when you're connected to the internet.
