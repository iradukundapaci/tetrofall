NoSleep Studios ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Tetrofall mobile game ("the App") collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

  • Device information — such as device model, operating system version, and language settings, used to keep the game running correctly across different devices.
  • Advertising identifiers (where permitted) — used by our advertising partners to serve and measure ads, subject to your consent choices and applicable law.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics — technical data collected automatically when the App crashes or encounters an error, used to fix bugs and improve stability.
  • Gameplay analytics (if enabled) — aggregated, non-identifying data about how the game is played, used to understand which features are working and where players get stuck.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display ads within the App. Depending on your consent choices and the laws that apply to you, ads may be personalized (based on your interests and activity) or non-personalized.

Consent

Where required by law — including under GDPR in the EEA/UK and applicable US state privacy laws — we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present a consent form before any personalized advertising takes place. You can change your choice at any time through the consent options presented in the App.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — ad serving and measurement.
  • Google Play Services — core platform services the App relies on when installed from Google Play.

These services have their own privacy policies governing how they handle data; we encourage you to review them.

Your Choices

You can:

  • Accept or decline personalized ads where a consent choice is available.
  • Reset your advertising identifier at any time in your device settings.
  • Contact us with any privacy questions or requests, including data deletion requests.

Children's Privacy

Tetrofall is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
