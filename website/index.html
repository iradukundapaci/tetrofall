Tetrofall

Blocks fall from above. Rows rise from below.

A falling-block puzzle by NoSleep Studios, built around one twist: the floor pushes a new row upward on a timer. Clear rows to buy back space and see how long you last.

What makes it different

⬆

The floor rises

A new row pushes up from the bottom every 22 seconds when you start, and every 4.5 seconds by minute twelve. You are not filling a well, you are racing it.

◼

Clears cascade

Blocks above a cleared row fall independently instead of sliding down as one slab. Columns collapse at different speeds, and rows sometimes finish themselves. Each link in a chain adds another 50% to your multiplier.

▦

A big board

18 columns by 32 rows of playing space. No lives, no energy meter, no hidden timers between runs.

✋

Built for one thumb

Swipe to move, tap to rotate, swipe down to drop. Auto-repeat is tuned for fast play, and a ghost piece shows where the piece lands. Leave it on or switch it off in Settings.

🎲

An honest randomiser

All seven pieces, dealt from a shuffled bag, so there are no droughts and no unwinnable streaks. Full rotation system with wall kicks.

✈

Plays offline

No account, no login, no cloud save. The game runs without a connection; only the ads need one. Watching a rewarded ad for a second chance is always your choice.

How to play

  1. Swipe to move Drag left or right to slide the falling piece across the board.
  2. Tap to rotate A tap turns the piece, with wall kicks so it still fits against an edge.
  3. Swipe down to drop Send the piece straight down and take the next one immediately.

About

Tetrofall is made for short, repeatable sessions. There is no level select and no campaign to work through: one board, one run, and your own best score as the thing to beat.

That score can do more than sit on the menu. Switch on Adjust start speed to my best score in Settings and each run begins further along the difficulty curve, so the game keeps pace with you instead of replaying the easy opening every time. Leave it off and every run starts from the same place. Your score and settings live on your device and nowhere else.
