Tetrofall

An endless block puzzle game by NoSleep Studios. One board, no finish line — just how long you can keep clearing lines.

Features

◼

Endless gameplay

No levels, no lives — one continuous run against the rising board.

🏆

Global high scores

See how your best run stacks up against players everywhere.

✋

Smooth controls

Responsive touch input tuned for fast decisions under pressure.

✈

Offline play

Core gameplay works without a connection; ads require internet to load.

🎬

Optional rewarded ads

Watch a short ad for a second chance to continue a run — entirely your choice.

About

Tetrofall is designed for quick, replayable sessions where players continually chip away at their own high score rather than working through a fixed set of levels. Every run is a fresh attempt at beating your last one.
